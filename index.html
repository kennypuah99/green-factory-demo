    How this agent decides
    
    
    
      
      
        
    • Energy per unit = Energy_kWh ÷ Output_Units. Flagged when 25% above the factory average.
      • 
        
    • Operator notes that match a known problem raise a warning even when the numbers still look normal.
      • 
        
    • Scrap rate = Scrap ÷ (Output + Scrap). Flagged above 4%.
      • 
        
    • Downtime flagged when a machine has more than 1.5× the average downtime.
      • 
        
    • Notes are scanned for keywords such as leak, warm-up, moisture, feeder to suggest a likely cause.
      • 
        
    • Savings: 50% of extra energy above average, 25% of scrap value, 20% of downtime cost.
      •
